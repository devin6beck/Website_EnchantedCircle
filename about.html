Our story

This was never just about
keeping a house clean.

When I started cleaning homes in Taos nearly ten years ago, the job felt simple: show up, do good work, and leave a home a little better than I found it. But when you care for the same homes year after year, you get to know them. You notice what’s out of place, which door sticks, which plants need a little extra water, and how someone likes things to feel when they come home.

Soon, clients were asking me to help while they were away, too. Check on the house after a storm. Meet a repair person. Get things ready for their return. Take care of whatever came up.

The answer was usually yes.

That’s how Enchanted Circle Home Care & Concierge came to be. Today, my team and I care for homes throughout Northern New Mexico, offering the kind of local, personal support that comes from truly knowing a home.
