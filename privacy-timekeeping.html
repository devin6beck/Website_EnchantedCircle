Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

01 / Who we are

About this policy

Enchanted Timekeeping is an internal timekeeping app for the staff of TAOS BEST CLEANING LLC, operating as Enchanted Home Care (“we”, “us”). It is not a public app: only people we employ and invite can use it. It is available as an Android app and in a web browser at staff.enchantedhomecare.com; this policy covers both.

This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who handles it, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email us at contact@TaosBestCleaning.com.

02 / What we collect

Information the app collects

  • Your account details: your name, email address and role (employee, manager or admin). If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive the name and email address of that Google account.
  • Work time records: clock-in and clock-out times, the tasks and properties you work on, breaks, and any timecard change requests, including corrections an admin makes and the reason given.
  • Schedules and job checklists assigned to you or your team, and which checklist items you tick.
  • Job notes: text and web links that you or other staff post on a scheduled job. Notes are visible to our active staff and are kept with their edit history.
  • Sick leave: the dates and hours of sick time you request, your balance, and the decision on each request. We do not ask for, or record, the medical reason for an absence.
  • Location, only at specific moments: the app records your location only when you clock in, when you clock out, and when you view a property entry code. It never tracks your location continuously or in the background.
  • A device ID that the app creates itself and attaches to your time records, so we can tell which phone a record came from.
  • Pay and billing information that admins enter for payroll and client billing: pay rates (including Training and sick-pay rates), estimated pay and approved hours.
03 / Why

How we use it

We use this information only to run our business:

  • keeping accurate time records;
  • running payroll, including paid sick leave;
  • scheduling work and sharing job notes with the team;
  • billing our clients for the work done at their properties; and
  • protecting our clients’ property entry codes, so they are only shown to staff at the right place.
04 / Sharing

Who handles your information

We use these service providers, which process data on our behalf and only on our instructions:

  • Supabase, which hosts the app’s database and servers in the United States;
  • Resend, which sends invitation emails and alert emails; and
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the browser version of the app.

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google handles that sign-in under its own privacy policy. Google may also review the Android app before it is published, using a test account we provide; that account can see the same information an admin can.

Within our company, managers and admins see the information their role needs, such as team schedules and timecards; admins can also see the location recorded when someone clocks in or out.

We do not sell your information. The app shows no advertising and uses no analytics or tracking services. Optional phone notifications are created and shown on your device only.

05 / Security

How we protect it

  • All information sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Records waiting to upload, for example when you have no signal, are kept in an encrypted database on your phone until they are sent.
  • The browser version works only while online. Your browser keeps your sign-in and any submission that has not yet been confirmed, so it can be checked safely; sign out on shared computers.
  • Access is limited by role: employees, managers and admins can each see only what their role needs.
06 / Retention

How long we keep it

We keep work, pay and sick-leave records for as long as payroll and tax laws require us to, and then delete them. Job notes and schedules are kept while they are useful to run the business and are deleted with your account unless they are part of those required records.

07 / Your choices

Your choices and rights

  • Location: you can decline to share your location when clocking in or out. You can still clock in or out, but the record will be flagged for a manager to review. Location is required to view property entry codes.
  • Alerts: you can turn alert emails and phone notifications off in the app under Account → Alert settings.
  • Your data: you can ask to see or correct your information by emailing contact@TaosBestCleaning.com.
  • Deleting your account: email contact@TaosBestCleaning.com with the subject “Delete my Enchanted Timekeeping account” from the email address you use in the app. We will confirm the request and, within 30 days, delete your sign-in account, contact details, job notes you wrote and location records that are not part of a required record. Time, pay and sick-leave records that payroll and tax laws require us to keep are retained for the legally required period and then deleted.
08 / Children

Children

The app is for adult employees only. It is not intended for, or offered to, children.

09 / Changes

Changes to this policy

If the way the app uses information changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date at the top.
