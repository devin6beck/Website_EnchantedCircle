FAQs

A few answers to common questions about our services and how we care for your home.

Ask us a question↗
01 / Pricing

How much do your services cost?

Our services are billed hourly, with rates varying by location. Contact us for pricing specific to your property.

02 / Private home care

Do you manage vacation rentals like Airbnb or VRBO?

No, we specialize in caring for private homes and second homes. By getting to know you, your property, and your preferences, we help make every visit to Northern New Mexico as comfortable, relaxing, and stress-free as possible. Our goal is for you to spend less time worrying about your home and more time enjoying it.

03 / Getting started

Do you offer one-time deep cleans?

No, we focus on ongoing home care rather than one-time cleanings. We begin with a property walkthrough to understand your needs and establish a customized service plan. From there, we provide regularly scheduled cleanings, property check-ins, and additional services as needed.
